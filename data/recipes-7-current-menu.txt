# More cocktails and batches.

@ Classics

* Negroni | dof | Stirred | Large cube | Orange twist
- 1 oz Gin
- 1 oz Campari
- 1 oz Sweet vermouth
> Stir down in a mixing glass and strain over a large rock.
! Menu: Gin, Campari, vermouth blend.
! Batch (per liter): 16 oz sweet vermouth, 16 oz Campari. Pour 2 oz batch + 1 oz gin.

* Whiskey Sour | dof | Shaken | Cubes | 2 Angostura strips
- 2 oz Bourbon
- 1 oz Lemon juice
- 1 oz Sour syrup
> Shake hard with 4-5 cubes, a little longer than usual to whip the foam.
> Dirty dump into a DOF and top with 2 fresh cubes.

* Freezer Martini | coupe | Batched, frozen | | Lemon twist and/or olive
- 2 3/4 oz Gin
- 5/8 oz Dolin dry vermouth
- 5/8 oz Filtered water
- 1 dash Orange bitters
> Pour 4 oz straight from the freezer bottle into a coupe. It leaves a small gap at the top.
> For a dirty martini, put 1/4-1/2 oz Dirty Sue in the coupe first.
! Menu: Gin, dry vermouth blend, lemon twist and/or olive. Served ice cold.
! Batch (per liter): 22 oz gin, 5 oz Dolin dry, 5 oz filtered water, 6-8 dashes orange bitters. Keep one bottle in the freezer and 2 deli containers to refill it. The single-drink amounts above are rounded from the batch.

* Dirty House Martini | coupe | Stirred | | 2-3 olives
- 2 1/2 oz Four Pillars Olive Leaf gin
- 1/2 oz Veso fig leaf vermouth
- 1/4 oz Dirty Sue olive brine
> Stir way down until ice cold in a mixing glass and strain into a coupe.
! Menu: Olive leaf gin, fig leaf, olives.
! Batch (per liter): 25 oz Four Pillars Olive Leaf gin, 5 oz Veso fig leaf vermouth. Pour 3 oz batch + 1/4 oz Dirty Sue.

* Martini | coupe | Stirred | | Lemon twist, olive, or both
- 2 1/2 oz Gin or vodka
- 1/2 oz Dry vermouth
> Stir until ice cold and strain into a coupe.
! Garnish with a lemon twist, an olive, or both.
! This is a 5:1 recipe. If it's too boozy, try a 3:1 ratio.
