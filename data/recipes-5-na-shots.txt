* Turning Violet | coupe | Whip shake | Crushed | Lime leaf
- 2 1/2 oz Kaffir lime leaf coconut cream
- 1 1/2 oz Orange juice
- Float 3/4-1 oz Blueberry syrup
> Whip shake with half a scoop of ice and dump into a short stemmed glass.
> Add fresh crushed ice on top and float the blueberry syrup.
! Menu: Creamy yet light. Not too sweet, not too savory.
