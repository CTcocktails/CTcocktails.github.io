* Grapefruit Cordial | bottle | Prep | |
- 6-8 Grapefruits
- Sugar, about equal to the juice
> Zest or peel 6-8 grapefruits.
> Hand squeeze the spent grapefruits into the peels.
> Add roughly the same amount of sugar as juice and give it a quick stir.
> Cover and refrigerate at least 24 hours before straining.

* Lime Cordial | bottle | Prep | |
- 12 Limes
- Sugar, equal to the juice
- 7-10 Lime leaves
> Peel 6 limes with a Y-peeler and zest the other 6 with a microplane.
> Hand squeeze the spent limes and add sugar equal to the juice.
> Smack and bruise the lime leaves and add them to macerate.
> Let sit 24 hours.

* Meyer Lemon Cordial | bottle | Prep | |
- About 12 Large Meyer lemons
- 8-10 cracks Black pepper
- Sugar, about equal to the juice
> Y-peel the lemons. If they're very soft, cut them in half and muddle everything together.
> Add the black pepper and the sugar, stir and refrigerate 24 hours.
! You want it very tart but a little thick.

* Mint Syrup | bottle | Prep | | 
- Fresh mint
- 2 parts Sugar
- 1 part Water
> Blanch the mint in boiling water for 15 seconds, then move it to ice water to stop the cooking.
> Make a rich simple syrup (2:1 sugar to water) over medium heat until the sugar dissolves.
> Blend the blanched leaves (no stems) into the syrup until the mint breaks up.
> Strain through a cold brew bag to remove any leaf residue.
~ Optional: a few dashes of Fee's mint bitters to retain color and freshness.

* Hazelnut Orgeat | bottle | Prep | |
- 2 cups Raw hazelnuts (skinless if possible)
- Water (2 cups per 1 cup of nuts)
- 16 oz Turbinado sugar per 12 oz hazelnut milk
- 4-5 dashes Rose water per 2 cups orgeat
- 1/4 oz Cognac per 2 cups orgeat
> Soak the hazelnuts 20-45 minutes in cold water and strain. Don't rinse.
> Get them moderately dry, crush into big chunks and toast a few minutes until a little golden.
> Blend 1 cup of nuts with 2 cups of water. Strain through a nut milk bag and let it sit a few minutes before squeezing.
> Mix every 12 oz of hazelnut milk with 16 oz turbinado over low heat, stirring constantly.
> When the sugar dissolves, simmer 5 minutes and take it off the heat.
> When cool, store in a cambro and stir in the rose water and cognac.
! Shelf life: 2 months.
! Works for most nuts with small tweaks. Take your time, you want as much oil as you can get for a richer syrup.
! If nuts have skins, gently scrub or peel after soaking. If it's thin, add more sugar; it thickens as it cools.
! Use rose or orange flower water and cognac sparingly, just enough to lift the nut. For almond orgeat, orange flower water is nice; add amaretto equal to the cognac.

* Pineapple Shrub | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 Pineapple
- Sugar, equal to the chopped pineapple
- 4-5 oz Champagne or white wine vinegar
> Cut off the ends, shave off all the skin and cut out the core.
> Dice the pineapple as small as possible and add an equal amount of sugar.
> Add just enough vinegar to dampen the sugar and speed up maceration.
> Muddle to mix, cover and refrigerate 24 hours, agitating now and then.
> Once the sugar is dissolved, fine mesh and muddle all the syrup out.
! It should smell lightly of vinegar but taste sweeter.

* Oleo Saccharum | bottle | Prep | |
- 12 Lemons
- 1 lb Sugar (about 2 cups, weigh it)
- 12 oz Boiling water
- 16 oz Fresh lemon juice, strained
> Peel the lemons with as little pith as possible.
> Vacuum seal the peels with the sugar, evenly spread, and let sit overnight.
> Juice the 12 lemons into a separate container.
> Pour the boiling water over the saccharum through the lemon pulp and stir to dissolve the sugar.
> Add the lemon juice and stir.
! Makes a sherbet ready for punches or cocktails.

* Roasted Persimmon Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- Persimmons
- Sugar, equal to the purée (3 cups purée = 3 cups sugar)
- Water, 1 cup more than the sugar (3 cups sugar = 4 cups water)
- Cinnamon sticks, broken
> Core out the tops and roast at 400°F about 15 minutes, until golden.
> Blend on high with a little water until fully smooth.
> Add the sugar, water and cinnamon and simmer 10 minutes at 205°F.
> Let sit covered 20 minutes, then strain through a chinois. Label and date.

* Strawberry Syrup | bottle | Prep | |

- 1 liter Water
- 1 liter Sugar

* Syrup Calculator (any juice) | bottle | Prep | |
- Juice, weighed in grams
- Sugar, calculated below
> Weigh the juice in grams and measure its Brix (%).
> Sugar already in the juice = juice weight × Brix.
> Juice without sugar = juice weight − that sugar.
> For 1:1 syrup, add (juice without sugar − sugar already in it) grams of sugar.
> For 2:1 syrup, add (2 × juice without sugar − sugar already in it) grams of sugar.
> Heat gently over low heat, stirring, until the sugar dissolves. Cool, bottle and refrigerate.
! Example: 1905 g juice at 10% Brix → 1524 g sugar for 1:1, 3238.5 g for 2:1.
