* Watermelon Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 pint Watermelon juice
- Sugar, 80% of the juice by weight
> Blend until the sugar dissolves.
! Used in Rosa.

* Thai Chili Apero | bottle | Prep | |
- Apero (Aperol-style aperitivo)
- 500 g Thai chilies, dehydrated
> Vacuum bag and run the infuse cycle twice.
> Strain through a coffee filter.
~ If it's too spicy, cut it with more Apero or Aperol. You want a kick, not a burn.

* Verde Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 8 oz Cucumber juice
- 1/2 oz Crème de menthe
- 3/4 oz Chareau aloe liqueur
- 3/4 oz Chinola passionfruit liqueur
- 4 3/4 oz Sugar
> Immersion blend until the sugar dissolves.
! Large batch: 40 oz cucumber juice, 2 3/4 oz crème de menthe, 3 oz Chareau, 4 oz Chinola, 28 oz sugar. Immersion blend until smooth.

* Cold Brew Concentrate | bottle | Prep | |
- 200 g Coffee
- 1 liter Water
> Stir or shake well to incorporate the grounds (loose, not in a coffee bag).
> Steep 24 hours at room temperature.
> Filter through coffee bags after steeping.
! Makes 1 liter. Triple everything for 3 liters.

* Cold Brew Vodka | bottle | Prep | |
- 150 g Coffee, weighed into cold brew bags
- 2 liters Vodka
- 1 liter Water
> Mix the vodka and water, then add the coffee in its cold brew bags.
> Steep 24 hours in the fridge.
> Pull out the bags and squeeze gently, wearing gloves.

- 8 oz Vanilla liqueur
! Per liter. For 3 liters: 48 oz cold brew, 24 oz salted coffee liqueur, 24 oz vanilla liqueur. Used in the Espresso Martini (2 1/4 oz batch).

* Caramel Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 liter Sugar (to the 1000 ml blender line)
- 500 ml Hot water
- 250 ml Water
- Disaronno or amaretto, 1/3 the amount of syrup
> Blend the sugar on high, pulsing about 1 minute to break it up.
> Add the hot water and blend another minute to dissolve.
> Boil in a saucepan about 15 minutes, until lightly golden brown.
> Take off the heat and stir in the 250 ml water to thin it back out.
> Finish with amaretto (for example 24 oz syrup to 8 oz amaretto) and stir to incorporate.

* Sour Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 9 g Methylcellulose
- 300 g Boiling water
- 600 g Cold water
- 900 g Sugar
> Stir the methylcellulose into the boiling water to dissolve.
> Immersion blend the cold water and slowly pour in the hot mixture (2+ minutes).
> Freeze 10 minutes to let the foam settle.
> Add the sugar and whisk or immersion blend on low.
> Strain off the foam and refrigerate.
! The foaming agent for the sours (Summer Trip, Disco Sour, Whiskey Sour).

* Black Tea Turbinado Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 cup Water
- 5 Black tea bags
- 500 g Turbinado sugar
> Steep the tea bags until dark and bitter (about 20 minutes).
> Combine 500 g turbinado with 250 g of the tea concentrate.
> Immersion blend 2 minutes until smooth.
! 2:1 sugar to tea concentrate by weight. Used in the Old Fashioned.

* Lime Leaf Agave | bottle | Prep | |

* Strawberry Rhubarb Cordial | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 part Rhubarb, chopped
- 1 part Sugar
- 1 part Water
- Dolin Chambéryzette, equal parts to the finished syrup
> Slow simmer the rhubarb, sugar and water just below a boil, about 15 minutes, until the sugar dissolves and the rhubarb is soft.
> Blend on high and strain through a coffee filter or cheesecloth.
> When it cools, add equal parts Chambéryzette (don't add it hot or it cooks the vermouth).
! Used in Summer Trip.

* Pineapple Coconut Cream | bottle | Prep | |
- 1 can Coco López (15 oz)
- 15 oz Simple syrup (refill the can)
- 1 can Dole pineapple juice (8 1/2 oz)
> Warm the can of coconut cream to soften the solids.

! Fills 2 cheater bottles.

* Cinnamon Syrup | bottle | Prep | |
- 6-10 Cinnamon sticks
- 1 part Water
- 1 part White sugar
> Break the cinnamon sticks with a muddler.
> Add equal parts water and sugar and simmer until the sugar melts.
> Take off the heat and steep 10-15 minutes.
> Strain through a coffee filter, or cheesecloth to remove all the cinnamon particles.
! Other version: 6-8 cracked cinnamon sticks per 16 oz boiling water, equal parts sugar, steep 20-60 minutes, strain and bottle.

* Upcycled Citrus Cordial | bottle | Prep | |
- Juice of all peeled, scored or old citrus
- White sugar, 80% of the juice by weight
- 1 barspoon Lactic acid
> Juice and blend all the leftover citrus.
> Add the sugar and lactic acid and immersion blend until the sugar is incorporated.
! Other version: equal parts sugar by weight, no lactic acid.

* Strawberry Vodka | bottle | Prep | |
- 6-7 Strawberries
- 1 bottle Vodka
> Mush the strawberries in a vacuum bag (by hand outside the bag, or with a muddler).
> Add the vodka and run the infuse cycle 3 times.
> Pour into a coffee filter and squeeze out all the juice (wear gloves).

- 1 bottle Rum
> Vacuum bag the strawberries and rum, run the infuse cycle 2-3 times, then strong seal.
> Store in the bag in the fridge, inside a flat cambro in case it breaks open.
